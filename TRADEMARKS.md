# Trademark policy

"Rustybin", the Rustybin logo and "Rustybin Cloud" (the **Marks**) identify the
official project and the products and services of its maintainer. The software
license (AGPL-3.0 or commercial) does not grant any right to use the Marks.

This policy aims to let the community talk about and build on Rustybin freely,
while making sure people are never confused about what is official.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, gateway recipe, tutorial or service works with, uses,
  or is built on Rustybin.
- Use the name "Rustybin" to refer to the project in articles, talks, books,
  documentation and social media.
- Distribute unmodified copies of the official releases under the name
  "Rustybin".
- Run community meetups or user groups that use the name, provided it is clear
  they are not run by the official project.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a fork, a modified distribution, or a product or hosted service
  "Rustybin" or anything confusingly similar (for example "Rustybin Pro",
  "RustybinHQ", "Rusty Bin Cloud").
- Use the Marks in a company, product or domain name.
- Use the Marks in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership or
  certification by the project when there is none.
- Modify the logo, or use it for anything other than referring to the project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the AGPL. A fork distributed or hosted publicly must use
a different name and logo, and may say "based on Rustybin".

Contact the maintainer, [@oliwynn](https://github.com/oliwynn) on GitHub, or open an issue titled "Trademark question".
